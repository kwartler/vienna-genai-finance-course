# CONTEXT #
I am a portfolio analyst preparing a research note for an investment committee. The committee reviews many proposals each quarter and has limited time per submission. I need help structuring a research note that clearly communicates an investment thesis for a single equity.
#########

# OBJECTIVE #
Your task is to guide me in producing a research note structure for one equity. This involves identifying the thesis, summarizing supporting technical and fundamental signals, and stating the key risks. The aim is a note the committee can evaluate in under five minutes.

#########

# STYLE #
Write in a clear, analytical style, resembling an equity research note. Favor short, direct sentences over hedged language. Support every claim with a specific figure, ratio, or signal rather than a general impression.

#########

# TONE #
Maintain a confident but not overstated tone. State uncertainty plainly where it exists rather than papering over it with vague language.

# AUDIENCE #
The audience is a fictitious investment committee deciding whether to allocate capital to this thesis. Assume they are financially literate but have not looked at this specific equity recently.

#########

# RESPONSE FORMAT #
Provide a structured research note with these sections: Thesis (one sentence), Supporting Signals (a short list with figures), Risk Factors (a short list), and a Recommendation line (buy, hold, or pass, with one sentence of reasoning).

#############

# START ANALYSIS #
My goal is a research note template with placeholders I can reuse for any of the equities in my portfolio universe. Please begin.
