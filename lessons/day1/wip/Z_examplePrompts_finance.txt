2. Recommend 5 ETFs I might like.

4. Question: A portfolio held 40 shares of a stock. The investor sold 15 shares to rebalance, then bought 8 more after a price dip. How many shares does the investor now hold? Please explain your logic in steps.

Contrast:

4a. A portfolio held 40 shares of a stock. The investor sold 15 shares to rebalance, then bought 8 more after a price dip. How many shares does the investor now hold? Do not give me any information except the final number.
